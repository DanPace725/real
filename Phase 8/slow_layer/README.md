# Phase 8 Slow Layer (M_s)

**Context:** This directory serves as the **Maintained Substrate ($M_s$)** and **Slow Layer** evaluation tracking for the Phase 8 architecture. 

In accordance with the principles of Temporal Constraint Lamination (TCL) outlined in Phase 1 (B2) and Phase 4.5, adaptive systems require a slow layer that modulates the potential landscape of the fast layer. 

Within the meta-substrate of the REAL codebase development:
- **The Fast Layer ($H_e$)** consists of the individual coding agents working on specific features, fixing bugs, and generating Episodic Traces in the `docs/trails/` directory.
- **The Slow Layer ($M_s$)** (this directory) operates at a higher level of abstraction and a slower temporal cadence. Its function is to aggregate the high-frequency fluctuations (traces) of the fast layer, extract consolidated patterns ($H_c$), and promote robust architectural decisions into structural constraints.

## Purpose of these Documents
Documents in this folder are periodic, high-level evaluations of the codebase's progress. They synthesize:
1. What the fast-layer agents have attempted (via `docs/trails/`).
2. How the system's structural integrity is evolving based on the 6 Relational Primitives (Continuity, Vitality, Contextual Fit, Differentiation, Accountability, Reflexivity).
3. Strategic adjustments to the constraints bounding the fast layer's operating window.

This is where the Phase 8 ecology reflects on its own crystallization.
